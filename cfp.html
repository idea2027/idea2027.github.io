Call for Papers

IDEA invites work on model-facing item identifiers in Web retrieval and ranking, spanning search, recommendation, and advertising. We welcome completed methods as well as concrete operational experiences, early results, and questions that benefit from community discussion.

Topics of Interest

Designing and learning

How should item IDs be constructed and used?

  • Raw, semantic, and hybrid identifier designs.
  • Content, collaborative signals, and multimodal ID construction.
  • Joint learning of identifiers and retrieval or ranking models.
  • Information sharing, item-specific distinctions, and feature-based alternatives.

Evaluating and comparing

What evidence supports an identifier choice?

  • Controlled comparisons and attribution of gains to ID design.
  • Reproducibility studies and negative results.
  • New-item and long-tail performance across search and recommendation.
  • Memory, latency, and update costs.

Deploying and evolving

How should IDs be maintained as items and models change?

  • Catalog updates, new items, and representation stability.
  • Encoder changes, code refreshes, and index maintenance.
  • Historical interactions and correct item resolution.
  • Coordinating representation updates with model retraining.

Submission Guidelines

We welcome technical studies, controlled comparisons, reproducibility work, negative results, deployment experiences, and position statements. Contributions should make a concrete identifier-design decision, comparison, or failure case clear.

  • Review: the working plan is at least three non-conflicted Program Committee reviews per submission, with an unconflicted review lead handling disagreements and organizer submissions.
  • Proceedings: eligible accepted papers may enter the Companion Proceedings, subject to the conference’s publication conditions.
  • Non-archival contributions: a separate route for position statements and discussion contributions is planned.
Submission details to be announced

Paper lengths, formatting instructions, anonymity policy, submission platform, and final publication policies will be published when confirmed. Submissions are not open yet.
