Workshop Format

Schedule to be announced

The workshop is planned as a half-day, in-person event. The exact date, session times, room, and presentation allocations are not yet confirmed.

A half-day, in-person workshop is planned, with substantial time for audience participation.

  • Keynote talks connecting research findings with deployment perspectives.
  • Contributed presentations including technical talks, lightning talks, and demonstrations.
  • Poster exchanges supporting informal conversations during the coffee break.
  • Brainstorming and open discussion comparing evidence and identifying useful next experiments.

Planned Sessions

  1. Welcome and framing

    Introduce the questions connecting ID construction, evaluation, and catalog evolution.

  2. Invited keynotes

    Research and deployment perspectives from the invited speakers.

  3. Contributed work and poster exchanges

    Technical and lightning talks, demonstrations, and informal discussion of accepted work.

  4. Moderated brainstorming

    Speakers, authors, and attendees compare interpretations, challenge assumptions, and propose follow-up experiments.

  5. Community Roadmap and closing

    Identify research priorities, record open disagreements, and gather resources for collaboration.
