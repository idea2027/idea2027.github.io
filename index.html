Overview

The First International Workshop on Item IDentifiers Design, Evaluation, and Adaptation (IDEA) brings together researchers and practitioners working on raw, semantic, and hybrid IDs for search, recommendation, advertising, and other Web systems.

We focus on identifier design and learning, evaluation and comparison, and deployment and adaptation. The half-day, in-person workshop will feature keynote talks, contributed presentations, and open discussion. Other applications and explorations of ID-based features in Web systems are also welcome.
